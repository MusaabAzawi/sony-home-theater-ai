Room Acoustic Analyzer

Analyze your listening room and get personalized EQ settings optimized for your Sony home theater system.

📋 Setup Instructions

  1. 1

    Position Your Microphone

    Place your phone or laptop 1–2 meters from the main speakers, at ear level in your primary listening position (e.g., center of the couch).

  2. 2

    Set Sony Volume to Reference Level

    Set your Sony receiver volume to your normal listening level. Disable any existing EQ, surround modes, or room correction (Dolby Atmos, DTS:X are fine).

  3. 3

    Play Test Tone or Music

    Use the built-in frequency sweep below, or play full-range music through your Sony system. Minimize background noise during analysis.

  4. 4

    Proceed to Microphone Analysis

    Once the test tone is playing, click Continue to Analysis and grant microphone permission when prompted.

⚠️ Disclaimer: Results are AI-estimated based on frequency data from your device's microphone. For professional-grade calibration, consider Audyssey MultEQ or Dirac Live.
